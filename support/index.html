Pairing

How do we pair?

One of you taps Make my code and shares it. The other types it in under Enter their code and taps Pair. You only do this once.

The code says it has expired

Codes last ten minutes on purpose, so an old one shared in a message cannot be used later by someone else. Make a new one.

Can I pair with more than one person?

Not yet. Sweet Little Line is built for two. Dropping a line to a small group is coming.

How do we unpair?

Settings, then Unpair. It clears both lines and unlinks the two phones. Either of you can do it, and you can pair again whenever you like.

The widget

How do I put it on my lock screen?

Touch and hold your lock screen, tap Customise, tap the lock screen, then tap the area below the clock and choose Little Line. There is a home screen widget too, if you touch and hold your home screen and tap the plus button.

Their line has not appeared

Give it a moment first — the widget is refreshed by iOS, not by us, and that can take a few seconds. If it is still wrong:

  • Check you are both online.
  • Open the app once on the phone that is not updating. That nudges the widget.
  • Check Settings, Little Line, and make sure Background App Refresh is on.

Still stuck? Email us and say which of you set the line and roughly when.

Why does it say "2 days ago"?

Because that is when they last dropped a line. A line sticks around until it is replaced or until it clears itself, so an old one is not a bug.

Notifications

I am not being told when they drop a line

That notification is off by default, because the whole point is a glance rather than a buzz. Turn it on in Settings. You will also need to allow notifications for Little Line in your iPhone's own Settings.

Your data

How do I delete everything?

Settings, then Delete my data. It removes your account, your name, your settings, your lines and your side of the pairing. It cannot be undone. If you have already deleted the app, email us and we will do it for you.

What do you collect?

Very little, and no email address or phone number. The privacy page lists all of it.

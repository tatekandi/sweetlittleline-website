How it works

Three things, then it’s out of your way.

  1. Pair once

    One six-character code, typed in once. No email, no phone number, nothing to sign up for.

    K7M 2Q4

  2. Drop your line

    Up to forty characters. Short enough that you say the thing instead of circling it.

    Home by seven. Bring the good bread. 36/40
    Drop my line

  3. It lands

    On their lock screen widget, with how long ago you set it. No notification unless they want one.

    Tate

    Thinking of you today.

Lines can clear themselves after an hour, two, or four — or stay until you change them. Six quick lines you write once sit under the box for the ones you send most.

What it doesn’t do

  • ×No chatThere is nowhere to reply. A line is a glance, not an opening.
  • ×No historyYesterday’s line is gone. Only the one you set now exists.
  • ×No feedNothing to scroll, nothing to catch up on, nothing waiting for you.
  • ×No read receiptsYou won’t know whether they looked. That was always the honest part.

For two people apart all day, a glance replaces a text.

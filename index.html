One line, on their lock screen.

Sweet Little Line is a small, free iPhone app for two people. You each drop one short line — a status, a feeling, or a nudge about milk — and it appears on the other's lock screen.

No chat. No history. No feed. For couples who are apart all day, a glance replaces a text.

How it works

  • Pair once with a six-character code. No email, no phone number, no account to make.
  • Type up to forty characters and drop your line.
  • It lands on their lock screen widget, with how long ago you set it.
  • Let it clear itself after an hour, two, or four — or leave it until you change it.
